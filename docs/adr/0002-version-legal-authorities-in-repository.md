# 2026-07-12 — Version the GH legal authority library in GitHub

## Decision

Store the approved GH Real Estate legal research corpus under `legal/` in this private repository. Keep searchable text and machine-readable source metadata on the default branch for the ChatGPT GitHub connector. Keep the eight approved PDF bundles in the same current release so they can also be pinned as ChatGPT Project Sources.

## Reason

GitHub provides reviewable history, hashes, scheduled monitoring, and connector search. Pinned PDFs provide a stable dated baseline in every project chat. The two formats are complementary.

## Controls

- Official text controls over extracts and summaries.
- Legal sources and internal GH policy remain clearly separated.
- Candidate changes require human review and are never automatically promoted as current law.
- Current consolidated law, effective addenda, historical law, proposed rules, guidance, and withdrawn material remain separately labeled.
- No tenant records, signed leases, payment records, credentials, or PII belong in the legal corpus.

## Scope

The initial release is current through July 12, 2026 and is scoped to GH Real Estate operations at 9401 Nieman Road, Overland Park, Kansas. It is an internal research aid, not legal advice and not a claim that every authority for every possible real-estate transaction is included.
