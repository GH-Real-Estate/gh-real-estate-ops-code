# Accounting source gaps and unresolved applicability facts

The July 12, 2026 accounting release is a strong operational index for GH Real Estate's current fourplex. It is not a licensed reproduction of US GAAP, a complete tax research platform, or a representation that every future transaction is covered.

## FASB limitations

- No FASB Codification text is stored. Exact paragraph wording and cross-references require authorized live access.
- Topic relevance is mapped, but exact subtopic and paragraph applicability remains transaction-specific.
- Effective dates, transition provisions, pending content, and private-company alternatives must be rechecked for the reporting period.
- The registry does not establish that GH is required to issue US GAAP financial statements.

## Facts requiring verification

- Whether any lender, investor, grant, court order, insurance agreement, or contract requires GAAP statements.
- Latest federal tax classification and whether Forms 8832 or 2553 were ever filed.
- Unit-by-unit personal-to-rental conversion dates and fair market values at conversion.
- Purchase allocation among land, building, land improvements, and shorter-lived components.
- Asset-level placed-in-service dates, dispositions, prior depreciation, and renovation groupings.
- Loan principal history, escrow activity, cash-out tracing, and any refinancing costs.
- Whether deposits are refundable, nonrefundable, advance rent, or legally earned under each signed agreement.
- Whether tenant reimbursements include distinct nonlease goods or services.
- Whether any related-party vendor, owner reimbursement, personal use, or below-market transaction exists.
- Whether GH hires employees, enters short-term lodging, acquires another entity, adds investors, forms affiliates, enters a joint venture, uses derivatives, receives a government grant, or sells/refinances the property.

## Tax and legal limitations

- Tax-year forms, thresholds, mileage rates, bonus depreciation, information-return rules, and estimated-tax dates are volatile.
- Kansas and Overland Park sources in the accounting sourcebooks are summaries; use the `legal/` corpus and live official sources for legal action.
- Case-law coverage is not exhaustive.
- A signed lease can change the accounting evidence and timing but cannot override mandatory law.

## Automated discovery limits

- Weekly discovery covers 9 configured accounting publisher indexes. It does not search the FASB Codification, tax research platforms, private letter rulings, court dockets, local tax administration, or every publication that could affect a transaction.
- A `current` result means no new, modified, or comparison-eligible missing item was detected under the configured comparison policies and no degradation condition occurred. It does not advance the July 12, 2026 release, prove that a conclusion remains valid, or establish US GAAP or tax compliance.
- FASB results are metadata-only. Exact Codification text, subtopics, paragraphs, scope exceptions, effective dates, transition provisions, and private-company alternatives require authorized live access and qualified review.
- Recent-item IRS and Treasury indexes use `rolling-window` missing semantics. An older item leaving the published window is not classified as withdrawn, superseded, revoked, or missing.
- The monthly review calendar checks repository dates only. A green calendar result does not prove that live FASB, IRS, Treasury, Kansas, contractual, or entity facts were reviewed.
- Any source retrieval or parser `error` makes discovery `degraded`, even when the catalog marks that source noncritical; missing configuration degrades the domain only when the affected source is critical. It does not mean no accounting or tax authority changed. Preserve the approved baseline and prohibit a material currentness assertion until the source and impact are reviewed.
- Candidate branches and workflow artifacts are unapproved metadata and review evidence. They must not drive journal entries, tax positions, chart-of-accounts changes, financial statements, or automation.

## Resolution process

Document the missing fact, official evidence, reviewer qualifications, conclusion, effective period, and resulting journal or tax adjustment in a pull request. GAAP review requires a CPA or qualified accounting reviewer using authorized FASB access; tax review requires a tax professional for the applicable year. Update the registry and changelog only after review; do not silently overwrite a dated conclusion.
