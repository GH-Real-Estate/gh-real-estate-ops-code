# GH accounting policy and transaction matrix

This matrix is an operating index. It does not replace the signed lease, live law, authorized FASB research, a tax return position, or CPA review.

| Transaction | Clean book treatment | Primary GAAP locators | Federal tax / legal cross-check | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current-period rent | Recognize lease income; reduce receivable when collected on accrual books | ASC 842, 310, 326 | IRC §61; Schedule E; executed lease | Lease, invoice, tenant ledger, settlement |
| Advance or last-month rent | Liability until earned under clean accrual books; track book-tax difference | ASC 842, 405 | Tax generally includes advance rent when received | Lease terms, receipt, coverage period |
| Refundable security or pet deposit | Debit cash; credit tenant deposit liability; tenant subledger | ASC 305, 405 | Not income while returnable; K.S.A. 58-2543 and 58-2550 | Lease/addendum, receipt, subledger |
| Holding deposit | Liability while refundable or creditable; reclassify only when legal entitlement is fixed | ASC 405, 450, 842 | Substance and Kansas deposit definition control | Signed holding terms, timeline, disposition |
| Deposit retained for rent or damage | Reduce liability only when legal right and amount are supported; record income/recovery and repair/asset separately | ASC 405, 450, 842, 606, 360 | Retained amount and repair deduction may have different tax timing | Move-in/out records, itemization, invoices, notices |
| Deposit refund | Reduce liability and cash; retain proof of delivery and unclaimed-check follow-up | ASC 405, 305 | Kansas deadline and unclaimed-property process | Calculation, forwarding address, payment proof |
| Late, returned-payment, or delinquency fee | Separate lease/other income when enforceable and earned; do not net processor loss | ASC 842 or 606, 450 | Generally rental income; signed lease and Kansas enforceability control | Lease clause, trigger date, payment history |
| Utility reimbursement | Usually show reimbursement and utility expense gross unless net presentation is supported | ASC 842/606, 225 | Tenant-paid owner obligations generally affect rental income and expense | Bills, allocation method, lease clause |
| Processor settlement | Record gross tenant payment; record processor fee separately; reconcile net deposit | ASC 305, 310, 405, 842 | Form 1099-K is a reconciliation input, not taxability authority | Processor report, bank deposit, invoice/payment IDs |
| Uncollected rent | Accrual books: receivable and expected-credit-loss analysis; cash/tax books: no unreceived rent income | ASC 310, 326, 842 | Cash-method landlord generally has no bad-debt deduction for rent never included | Aging, collection history, lease status |
| Concession or rent credit | Record as lease consideration change according to substance and timing | ASC 842, 250 | Tax follows applicable income method; preserve agreement | Signed concession, dates, affected invoices |
| Repair and maintenance | Expense if it preserves ordinary operating condition and is not part of an improvement | ASC 360 | Treas. Reg. §§1.162-4 and 1.263(a)-3 | Invoice, scope, unit of property, related work |
| Betterment, restoration, adaptation, or renovation | Capitalize direct costs by asset/component; depreciate when placed in service | ASC 360, 835, 970 | Tangible-property regulations; tax lives may differ | Contract, invoices, permits, placed-in-service evidence |
| Appliance, furniture, or equipment | Capitalize under book policy unless a reviewed expensing threshold applies; asset subledger | ASC 360 | MACRS, de minimis, bonus, and §179 are separate tax analyses | Invoice, serial/asset ID, location, service date |
| Property acquisition and closing | Allocate purchase price and qualifying costs; land is nondepreciable | ASC 805 or asset-acquisition guidance, 360, 820, 970 | IRC basis rules and closing-cost classifications | Closing disclosure, appraisal/allocation, settlement invoices |
| Mortgage payment | Split principal, interest, escrow, fees, and suspense amounts | ASC 470, 835, 305, 340 | Principal nondeductible; interest and tracing fact-specific | Lender statement, amortization, bank settlement |
| Escrow funding/disbursement | Funding increases escrow asset; recognize tax/insurance when servicer pays | ASC 305, 340, 405 | Deduction timing follows underlying payment and method | Annual escrow analysis, county/insurer receipt |
| Loan origination or refinancing costs | Record and amortize qualifying debt issuance costs; separate lender/third-party and property costs | ASC 340, 470, 835 | Tax amortization and points rules may differ | Closing package, fee detail, loan terms |
| Owner contribution | Equity contribution, never rent or operating income | ASC 505, 850 | Generally not Schedule E income | Transfer proof, member authorization, purpose |
| Owner distribution/draw | Equity distribution, never operating expense | ASC 505, 850 | Not a Schedule E deduction; Kansas solvency/LLC rules | Authorization, equity ledger, bank proof |
| Owner-paid business expense | Record expense/asset with contribution or payable to owner according to policy | ASC 505, 850, relevant expense/asset topic | Must meet ordinary/necessary and substantiation rules | Invoice, business purpose, reimbursement status |
| Related-party purchase or service | Record substance and disclose relationship; assess arm's-length support | ASC 850, 450, 360 | Related-party tax rules and documentation may apply | Contract, relationship, pricing support, invoice |
| Vendor payment | Expense, prepaid, or asset based on substance; track information-return status | ASC 360/340/720 as applicable, 405 | Forms W-9, 1099-MISC/NEC, card/third-party exceptions | W-9, invoice, payment method, calendar-year total |
| Business mileage/travel | Record only supported business use under adopted reimbursement/accounting policy | ASC 720, 850 | IRC §274 and current mileage guidance | Contemporaneous log, origin/destination, purpose |
| Casualty or insurance recovery | Record loss, impairment, receivable, and gain only under applicable recognition thresholds | ASC 360, 450, 610 | IRC casualty/involuntary-conversion rules may differ | Damage report, adjuster, claim, settlement, repairs |
| Property sale or abandonment | Derecognize assets and related balances; record gain/loss and selling costs | ASC 360, 610, 470 | Forms 4797/6252, §§1231/1245/1250/453/1031 as applicable | Contract, closing statement, lifetime asset ledger |
| New property/entity acquisition | Determine asset acquisition versus business combination and consolidation scope before posting | ASC 805, 810, 820, 970 | Entity classification, basis, elections, and state filings | Purchase agreement, entity chart, valuation, debt |
| Employee/payroll | Accrue compensation and payroll liabilities; activate payroll controls | ASC 710, 715/718 if applicable, 405 | Federal/Kansas payroll, unemployment, workers compensation | I-9/W-4, payroll register, benefit documents |
| Government grant or assistance | Do not analogize automatically; identify grant terms and applicable GAAP model | ASC 105 plus issued grant guidance/effective dates | Taxability and basis reduction are program-specific | Award, compliance terms, expenditures, effective-date research |
| Software/tenant portal development | Distinguish preliminary, application-development, implementation, hosting, and maintenance phases | ASC 350-40 or 985 when applicable | Tax software/amortization rules may differ | Project plan, invoices, time records, go-live date |

## Posting control

Every material posting recommendation should identify:

1. Zoho account and transaction type.
2. Debit and credit.
3. Book basis and timing.
4. Tax difference and adjustment.
5. Tenant/vendor/property dimension.
6. Evidence attached outside GitHub.
7. Reviewer and escalation trigger.
