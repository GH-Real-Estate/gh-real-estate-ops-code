# FASB research and licensing policy

## Approved repository content

The repository may contain:

- ASC topic, subtopic, section, and paragraph locators that were independently recorded through authorized research.
- Original GH summaries and applicability notes.
- Transaction facts, conclusions, journal-entry examples, and book-tax differences written for GH.
- Official URLs, issue dates, effective-date notes, review dates, and reviewer identity.
- CPA-authored or CPA-approved position memoranda when the engagement and license permit repository storage.

## Prohibited repository content

Do not commit:

- Copied FASB Codification paragraphs, topics, subtopics, tables, implementation examples, or definitions.
- FASB print-to-PDF exports or screenshots.
- Bulk exports, scraped content, reconstructed Codification text, or a substitute searchable corpus.
- Commercial accounting guides, checklists, or templates unless the license expressly permits repository and generative-AI use.
- Credentials or session material used to access licensed accounting content.

Private repository status does not create permission. Git history can preserve a prohibited copy after an ordinary deletion.

## Research controls

1. Use authorized live FASB access for exact text.
2. Record only the minimum locator necessary for later verification.
3. Write the GH application analysis independently.
4. Record the reporting period, entity type, private-company status, effective date, elections, and assumptions.
5. Have a CPA review material recognition, measurement, impairment, lease-classification, acquisition, disposition, or tax-basis conclusions.
6. Store the final memo and approval—not the underlying copied Codification pages.
7. Recheck effective-date and transition sections before each reporting period and after each relevant ASU.

## Monitoring controls

- Subscribe to official FASB update notifications and monitor the official standards and projects pages through permitted methods.
- Do not scrape the Codification or use FASB site content as a generative-AI grounding corpus.
- Automation may validate registry structure, detect overdue reviews, and open issues.
- Automation may not declare an ASU applicable, revise an accounting policy, or merge a currentness change.

## If prohibited material is committed

1. Stop sharing or processing it.
2. Open a private security/legal review issue without quoting the material.
3. Remove it from the working tree and determine whether Git history must be rewritten.
4. Rotate credentials if any access information was exposed.
5. Obtain counsel or license guidance before restoring any portion.
