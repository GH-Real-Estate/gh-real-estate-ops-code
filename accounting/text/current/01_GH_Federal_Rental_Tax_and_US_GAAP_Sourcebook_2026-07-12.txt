1. Federal scope and controlling assumptions
This volume assumes GH Real Estate, LLC is a Kansas domestic single-member LLC that has not elected corporate treatment; the
owner reports ordinary long-term residential rental activity on the cash method; no employees or substantial tenant-convenience
services exist. Each assumption must be verified from filed documents and actual operations.
GAAP and tax are different systems
GAAP is an accrual financial-reporting framework. The Internal Revenue Code and Treasury Regulations control federal taxable income.
Cash basis and income-tax basis are other comprehensive bases of accounting, not GAAP. Maintain book-tax adjustments instead of
forcing one system to mimic the other.

3. Entity, return, and accounting method
 - Verify the latest Form 1040, Schedule E, EIN notice, Articles/operating agreement, and any Forms 8832 or 2553. A single-member
   domestic LLC is generally disregarded for federal income tax unless it elects corporation treatment.

 - For income-tax information reporting, the owner generally supplies the owner's legal name and TIN on Form W-9 line 1 and the
   disregarded LLC name on line 2. Employment and certain excise taxes treat the LLC separately.

 - Use the tax accounting method shown on the filed return unless a permissible method change is made. An internal Zoho setup does
   not elect or change a federal accounting method.

 - Ordinary fourplex rent with normal landlord services is generally Schedule E and excluded from self-employment income.
   Maid/linen/regular in-unit service or a separate management business can change the return and payroll analysis.

5. Repairs, improvements, and 2026 renovations
A single invoice amount does not decide classification. Analyze the unit of property and the entire plan of work. For a building, the
regulations separately test the building structure and systems such as HVAC, plumbing, electrical, elevators, fire protection, security,
gas distribution, and other listed systems. A replacement that materially improves, restores, or adapts the unit of property is capital
even when the contractor calls it a repair.
2026 control
Do not post the entire renovation program to a 'Renovations' expense account. Build an invoice-level schedule showing scope, related
project, building/system, unit, placed-in-service date, book treatment, federal tax treatment, Kansas treatment, and supporting source.

7. Loss limitations and QBI
 - Apply limitations in order: basis and at-risk rules; passive-activity rules; then excess-business-loss rules. Carry each limitation
   separately.

 - Normal rental real estate remains passive even with active management unless a statutory/regulatory exception applies. The
   active-participation allowance can be up to $25,000 and generally phases out between $100,000 and $150,000 of modified
   adjusted gross income, with special filing-status rules.

 - Real-estate-professional status requires more than 750 hours and more than half of the taxpayer's personal-service time in
   real-property trades/businesses, plus material participation in the rental(s). Do not infer it from job title or ownership.

 - Rental income is QBI only when the activity is a Section 162 trade/business or meets Revenue Procedure 2019-38. The safe harbor
   needs separate books, qualifying rental-service hours, contemporaneous records, exclusions, and an attached annual statement.

 - Passive-loss and QBI conclusions should be made on the individual return facts, not inside Zoho alone.

9. 2025-2026 changes and stale-source traps
 1. Permanent 100% bonus depreciation now applies to qualifying property acquired after January 19, 2025. The residential building
    and land do not qualify; shorter-life assets still require correct classification and passive-loss analysis.

 2. For payments made in 2026, the general information-return threshold is $2,000, not the $600 shown in 2025 Schedule E
    instructions. Form-specific exceptions remain.

 3. The third-party settlement organization Form 1099-K threshold is again more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions.
    Payment-card reporting is separate, and processors may issue a form below a threshold.

 4. The QBI deduction is permanent, but rental real estate is not automatically QBI. Do not rely on an outdated IRS web page that says
    Section 199A ended after 2025.

 5. The 2026 business mileage rate changed midyear: 72.5 cents through June 30 and 76 cents beginning July 1 under
    Announcement 2026-11.

 6. Domestic US-created LLCs are currently exempt from CTA BOI reporting. Older broad domestic-entity filing guides are obsolete.

 7. FinCEN's Residential Real Estate Rule was vacated March 19, 2026 and is under appeal. Recheck the live FAQ before any
    covered transfer.

 8. Publication 535 was discontinued after tax year 2022. Use the current IRS business-expense resource map and topic-specific
    publications.

 9. Never use a document marked DRAFT - NOT FOR FILING as current authority.


10. US GAAP applicability and topic map
No general federal rule identified requires this private, non-SEC, single-member fourplex landlord to issue GAAP financial statements.
GAAP can become mandatory through a lender covenant, investor agreement, court order, grant, insurance contract, or other specific
requirement. If GH chooses GAAP, it must apply the relevant Codification consistently and prepare the required
statements/disclosures for the stated reporting framework.
