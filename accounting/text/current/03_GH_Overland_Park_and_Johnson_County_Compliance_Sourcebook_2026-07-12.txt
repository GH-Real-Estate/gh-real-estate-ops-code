1. Local conclusion for the GH fourplex
The central current local obligation is Overland Park rental licensing and inspection under Municipal Code Chapter 5.75. The current
master fee schedule lists $120 billed biennially for a rental dwelling under common ownership and $120 per building for an apartment
complex. The license is not a substitute for construction permits, state taxes, property-tax filings, or a valid lease.
Immediate verification
Confirm the active rental license shows the correct fourplex address and legal owner, capture its effective/renewal dates, and store the
issued license and fee receipt. If ownership changed, Chapter 5.75 says the license is not transferable.

4. General business license and sales tax
Overland Park's business-license page lists specific regulated activities and does not identify a general occupational license for
ordinary long-term residential landlords. That does not remove the separate Chapter 5.75 rental license. Long-term apartment rent is
not Kansas sales-taxable. Kansas KDOR administers local sales taxes on taxable goods/services; unusual separately sold services or
taxable personal-property transactions require state/local rate review.

5. Johnson County real-property tax workflow
 - Real property is valued as of January 1. Multifamily residential classification is generally assessed at 11.5% under Kansas law.

 - The deadline to appeal the appraised value is generally 30 days after the value notice is mailed. Calendar the actual notice date and
   preserve comparable/property-condition evidence.

 - Taxes may be paid in full by December 20 or half by December 20 and half by May 10 of the following year. Weekend/holiday
   adjustments can change the calendar date.

 - When the lender pays through escrow, reconcile the lender statement to the county bill and receipt. Do not expense the monthly
   escrow deposit itself.

 - The county estimated-tax notice is informational and not the tax bill; do not post it as a payable or payment.


6. Personal-property workflow
Kansas changed the rendition rule effective January 1, 2025. File an initial personal-property rendition, then file by March 15 only when
a required change occurs. Johnson County's page should be read with current K.S.A. 79-306 and the 2026 KDOR guide. Appliances,
laundry machines, furniture, tools, and equipment need a real-versus-personal and exemption review; many assets may be exempt,
but a mistaken exemption can cause back tax and penalties.

8. Short-term lodging - not currently applicable
Trigger only
Current facts describe long-term apartment leases. If GH offers stays of 28 consecutive days or less, stop using the long-term conclusion
and re-scope before listing or collecting money.

Kansas guidance states transient guest tax generally applies when more than two bedrooms are furnished for guest accommodations
and the room is rented for 28 consecutive days or less. The official rate table lists Overland Park at 9.00% as of July 1, 2026. Kansas
sales tax lodging rules have their own sleeping-room and accommodation tests. City zoning, licensing, platform collection, and return
filing must all be verified before a short-term conversion.
