# FASB applicability index for GH Real Estate

**Checked through:** July 12, 2026  
**Scope:** Kansas single-member LLC operating one long-term residential fourplex  
**Content rule:** Locators and original GH analysis only; no Codification text

This index intentionally casts a wider net than GH's current transactions. A topic is included when it is either part of a defensible GAAP financial-statement baseline or could become relevant through a realistic acquisition, financing, staffing, ownership, lease, software, casualty, sale, or growth event.

## Status labels

- **Baseline** — review when preparing or claiming US GAAP financial statements.
- **Transaction-triggered** — review only when the identified fact or transaction exists.
- **Future-structure** — not expected under current facts, but activated by a material business-model or ownership change.
- **Currently not applicable** — retained to prevent accidental application and to define the trigger for reassessment.

## Private-company decision points

When GAAP statements are required, explicitly evaluate private-company alternatives and practical expedients that may affect Topics 350, 805, 810, and 842. Record:

- Eligibility as a private company.
- Election date and reporting period.
- Consistency requirements.
- Transition method.
- Financial-statement and disclosure consequences.
- Whether a lender or other user prohibits or expects a different election.

## Exclusions

Industry topics with no realistic connection to GH's present or foreseeable business—such as airlines, extractive activities, insurance, health care, casinos, broadcasting, and regulated utilities—are intentionally omitted. If the business model changes materially, perform a fresh whole-Codification scoping review rather than treating this index as immutable.
