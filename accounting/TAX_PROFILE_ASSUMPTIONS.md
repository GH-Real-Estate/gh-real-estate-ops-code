## Privacy boundary

Do not commit any of the following to GitHub:

- Names of spouses or employers when not operationally necessary.
- Home street address.
- W-2s, Forms 1099 containing taxpayer data, tax returns, transcripts, or payroll records.
- Wage, withholding, income, deduction, basis, or net-worth amounts tied to an identifiable taxpayer.
- SSNs, EINs, TINs, dates of birth, bank details, credentials, or tax-software exports.

Private evidence belongs in the CPA's secure portal or GH's approved private document system. GitHub may store only sanitized checklists, source locators, status metadata, and approved non-sensitive policy conclusions.
