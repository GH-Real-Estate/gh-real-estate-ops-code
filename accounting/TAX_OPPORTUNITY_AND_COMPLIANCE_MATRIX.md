# GH tax opportunity and compliance matrix

**Profile date:** July 13, 2026  
**Scope:** One Kansas disregarded single-member LLC, one long-term Overland Park fourplex, married Kansas household with spouse W-2 wages  
**Control:** This is a decision register, not a promise of tax savings or filing instruction.

The best tax outcome comes from complete facts, correct timing, defensible elections, and pre-transaction review. It does not come from adding more general-ledger accounts or automatically applying every deduction.

## Zoho Books control decision

Do not rebuild the current chart of accounts for tax optimization. The reconciled chart already has Schedule E mappings, asset and accumulated-depreciation accounts, repair/improvement separation, advance-rent and deposit treatment, nondeductible review accounts, and an authority crosswalk.

The practical controls are:

- Use the current account and property/unit dimensions consistently.
- Maintain an asset subledger with asset ID, description, property/unit, acquisition date, placed-in-service date, original cost, land/building/component allocation, book method, tax method, election status, disposition date, and proceeds.
- Require a `Repair / Improvement / Review` decision on material property work.
- Preserve vendor W-9 status, entity type, payment method, and 1099 status outside public reports.
- Preserve mileage and QBI rental-service time logs.
- Keep personal federal/Kansas income-tax payments outside the rental P&L.
- Export a CPA package; never force book accounting to equal the tax return.

## CPA package — minimum annual contents

1. Trial balance, general ledger, Schedule E mapping, and bank/clearing reconciliations.
2. Rent roll and year-end receivable, advance-rent, deposit-liability, and retained-deposit reconciliation.
3. Fixed-asset rollforward and book-versus-tax depreciation schedule.
4. Repair/improvement review list with invoices and scopes.
5. Mortgage principal, interest, escrow, refinancing, and lender statements.
6. Property-tax bills, valuation notice, appeal outcome, rental-license evidence, and personal-property determination.
7. Vendor payment report with W-9/1099/payment-method status.
8. Owner contributions, distributions, reimbursements, and related-party items.
9. Mileage log and rental-service time log.
10. Prior-return carryovers, filed elections, and unresolved items listed in `accounting/SOURCE_GAPS.md`.

## Review rule

A detected source change creates a review candidate. It never changes a Zoho account, tax code, transaction classification, election, payment, depreciation schedule, or return position automatically.
