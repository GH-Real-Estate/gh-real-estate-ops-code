# GH operational context — not law

This directory documents the boundary between GH Real Estate operational facts and the legal authority library. Operational policies, lifecycle files, and project summaries are intentionally excluded from the approved legal release. They may help determine which authorities are relevant, but they are not statutes, regulations, ordinances, court decisions, lease terms, or legal opinions.

Never treat an internal policy as proof of legal compliance. If a context document conflicts with binding law, an applicable signed agreement, or a court order, flag the conflict and use the controlling authority.
