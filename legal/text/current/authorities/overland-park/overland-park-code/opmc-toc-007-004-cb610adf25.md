7.16.100 Purpose and Intent.
 
A. Protect and encourage the protection and Maintenance of Trees and Shrubs.
B. Promote and preserve the City's aesthetic value with respect to Trees and Shrubs.
C. Regulate and control planting and Maintenance of Trees and Shrubs on and adjacent to 
private property, Right-of-way and other public properties.
D. Provide for public health, safety and general welfare with respect to Trees and Shrubs.
E. Establish the Legacy of the Greenery Committee as a tree board for the City.
F. Designate a City Forester.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §1, 2014; TP-2032 §1, 97; TP-1289 §1, 85)
7.16.110 Legacy of Greenery Committee.
 
A.Creation. 
There is hereby created and established a tree board for the City to be known as the Legacy 
of Greenery Committee (hereinafter referred to as the "Committee").
B.Purpose and Responsibility. The Committee shall be responsible for:

1. Developing strategies to increase, preserve and enhance the City’s Tree canopy by 
promoting, planting and maintaining Trees and Shrubs on both public and private

2. The promotion and recognition of commercial developments, businesses, and public

1. The Committee shall consist of 13 members who are residents of the City and not less

ex-officio) shall be appointed by the Mayor with the consent of the City Council. 
2. Should any Committee member move outside the City, the Committee shall declare to the 
Mayor the existence of a vacancy.
3. The appointments shall be made for two-year terms, plus until their successors take

of the City Council for the unexpired term.
4. Committee members shall serve on a volunteer basis without compensation from the City 
or any other organization, trust, donation, or legacy for their services to the City.
5. The Committee shall adopt by-laws consistent with the City Council’s Rules, Procedures

6. The Committee shall elect from its membership a chair and vice-chair, who shall serve one

7.16.130 Definitions.

For purposes of this Chapter the following definitions shall apply to the listed terms:
A.City Forester– means the City official designated in Section 7.16.120 to observe and enforce 
the provisions of this Chapter. All references to the City Forester shall include the City 
Forester’s designee(s).
B.Community Services Division– means the City office designated by the City Manager to 
perform the duties specified in this Chapter. All references to the Community Services 
Division shall include the Community Services Division’s designee(s), including but not 
limited to, the City Forester or any City contractor.
C.Landscape Improvements– means, for the purposes of this Chapter, landscape and 
hardscape outdoor improvements including, but not be limited to, retaining walls, statues, 
and decorative boulders.
D.Maintenance- means, without limitation, the protecting, spraying, fertilizing, treating, 
pruning, abatement of infestation and trimming of Trees or Shrubs.
E.Nuisance– means the doing of an act, failure to perform a legal duty, or the allowance or 
creation of a condition which injures, endangers, hinders or unreasonably interferes with the 
public health, safety or welfare in the use and enjoyment of Right-of-way or other public 
property by the general public. Examples include, but are not limited to, brush and 
limbs, Trees and Shrubs which are dead, diseased or infested which present a harmful or 
dangerous condition to the public, and encroachments of Trees and Shrubs in the Right-of-
way or other public property which create a safety hazard.
F.Owner– means any person, agent, operator, firm or corporation having a legal or equitable 
interest in the (real) property; or recorded in the official records of the Johnson County 
Register of Deeds office as holding title to the property; or otherwise having control of the 
property, including the guardian of the estate of any such person, and the executor or 
administrator of the estate of such person if ordered to take possession of real property by a 
court.
1.Abutting Owner – means an Owner that directly abuts the Right-of-way where 
the Tree or Shrub requiring Maintenance or removal is located.
G.Right-of-way– means the area on, below or above the present and future City Streets, 
alleys, bridges, bikeways, parkways and sidewalks.
H.Shrub– means a low, usually several-stemmed, woody plant. For the purposes of this 
Chapter, it shall also include ornamental and native grasses used in place of Shrubs.
I.Street– means the pavement and sub-grade of a City residential, collector or arterial 
roadway.
J.Tree– means a woody plant having a well-defined stem or trunk, a more or less definite 
crown, a height at maturity of at least eight feet, and the trunk diameter exceeds two inches.
1.Large Trees - Trees with a potential mature height of 50 feet or more.
2.Medium Trees - Trees with a potential mature height between 25 to 50 feet.
3.Small Trees - Trees with a potential mature height of no more than 25 feet.
4.Street Trees - Trees located within the Right-of-way.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §4, 2014; TP-2032 §3, 97; TP-1289 §4, 85)

7.16.150 Tree and Shrub Distance and Clearance Requirements.
 
A. No Tree or Shrub shall be planted in the Right-of-way within a distance of:
1. 10 feet of any fireplug
2. 15 feet of any regulatory sign
3. 20 feet of any street light pole
4. 10 feet of any box culvert
B. The minimum height clearances for Trees and Shrubs are:
1. 8 feet over public sidewalks
2. 13.5 feet over improved thoroughfares or designated truck routes
3. 12.5 feet over all other Streets
C. No Tree or Shrub shall be planted or maintained in the Right-of-way within any zone of 
approach, meaning, the area allowing an unobstructed view in advance of an actual or 
possible future traffic control device for a distance of 150 feet as measured from the point of 
the nearest intersecting curbs or curb lines of the Right-of-way.
D. The sight distance areas, as defined in OPMC Sections 12.04.126 and 18.450.070, shall remain 
free of Shrubs, Landscape Improvements or other vegetation which exceed a height of 2 feet 
measured from the Street. Trees in this area must be approved by the City Forester.
E. No Trees, Shrubs or Landscape Improvements over 2 feet in height will be permitted on 
residential traffic islands or thoroughfare Right-of-way unless approved by the City Forester.
F. Only Small Tree species, as designated in accordance with Section 7.16.180, may be planted 
or maintained within any portion of Right-of-way with overhead electric lines.
G. No Tree or Shrub shall be planted or maintained in a location that in any manner creates a 
Nuisance.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §5, 2014; TP-1866 §1, 94; TP-1784 §1, 93; TP-1289 §6, 85)
7.16.180 Approved Street Tree List.
 
Street Tree species allowed to be planted and grown within the Right-of-way will be set forth on the 
List of Approved Street Trees for Overland Park. This list will be made readily available through the 
City Forester and on the City’s web site and it can be mailed upon request. This list will be reviewed 
and updated by the Legacy of Greenery Committee at least every three (3) years to reflect changes 
found in arboricultural practices. The planting of any species not on this list, shall be prohibited 
unless prior written authorization of the City Forester is obtained; and the City Forester is 
authorized to adopt regulations providing procedures and criteria for the approval of species not 
on this List.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §6, 2014; TP-2732 §1, 2008; TP-2032 §5, 97; TP-1289 §9, 85)
7.16.190 Trees and Shrubs on Private Property.
 
A.Authority. The Community Services Division shall have the authority to regulate and enforce 
all regulations concerning the Maintenance and removal of Trees and Shrubs on private 
property. The City Forester shall assist the Community Services Division as necessary.

B.Maintenance or Removal. Whenever the Community Services Division or the City Forester 
determines that any Tree or Shrub on private property endangers, hinders or obstructs the 
use of public or private property, is a Nuisance, or in any other way endangers the public 
health, safety and welfare, the Community Services Division shall issue a notice of violation to 
order the Maintenance or removal of the Tree or Shrub by the Owner. (See Sections 7.16.205 
and 7.16.208.)
C.Emergency Action. In the event the Community Services Division or the City Forester 
determines that the existing condition of any Tree or Shrub on private property constitutes 
an immediate hazard to the public health, safety and welfare or otherwise endangers persons 
or property, then the City shall proceed, without delay, to take steps to abate the situation 
and without prior notice to or hearing of the Owner, occupant or agent. The cost of such 
action shall be assessed as set forth in O.P.M.C. Chapter 7.28 as amended, or pursuant to 
K.S.A. 12-1,115, as amended, or both.
D.Right to Inspect. The Community Services Division and the City Forester are hereby 
authorized to enter on private property for the purpose of inspecting Trees or Shrubs where 
there appears to be a Nuisance or a threat to public or private property or to the public, 
health, safety and welfare. This entry and inspection may be done without the prior 
permission of the Owner, occupant or agent if the threat is imminent and if such notification 
is not feasible under the circumstances. If any person prevents such entry and inspection, the 
City may seek an appropriate court order to make such entry and inspection possible.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §7, 2014; TP-2032 §7, 97; TP-1289 §11, 85)
7.16.195 Trees and Shrubs on Public Property.
 
A.Authority. The City Forester shall have the authority to regulate and enforce all regulations 
concerning the planting, Maintenance and removal of Trees and Shrubs on public property.
B.Maintenance.
1. Whenever the City Forester determines that Maintenance of any Trees or Shrubs located 
in any Right-of-way is necessary, the City Forester shall follow the procedures set forth in 
Section 7.16.200. Notwithstanding, the City shall always maintain the right to perform any 
necessary Maintenance of any Tree or Shrub in Right-of-way and the City Forester may at 
anytime, in the City Forester’s discretion, direct city staff or the City’s contractor to 
perform the Maintenance.
2. Whenever the City Forester determines that Maintenance of any Trees or Shrubs located 
in any public property other than Right-of-way is necessary, the City Forester shall direct 
city staff or the City’s contractor to perform the Maintenance. Notice to any Abutting 
Owner is not required.
C.Removal.
1. Whenever the City Forester determines that removal of any Shrubs located in any Right-
of-way is necessary, the City Forester shall follow the procedures set forth in Section 
7.16.200. Notwithstanding, the City shall always maintain the right to perform any 
necessary removal of any Shrub in Right-of-way and the City Forester may at anytime, in 
the City Forester’s discretion, direct city staff or the City’s contractor to perform the 
removal.

7.16.200 Abutting Owner’s Duty to Right-of-way.
 
A.Duty. Abutting Owners are responsible for Maintaining the Trees and Shrubs within or 
encroaching within the abutting Right-of-way in a manner which promotes safe and healthy 
Trees and Shrubs and which protects the health, safety, and welfare of the public with 
respect to the same. The Abutting Property Owners shall not allow such Trees and Shrubs to 
hinder or obstruct the Right-of-way, create a Nuisance, or threaten life or property within the 
Right-of-way. When applicable, this duty shall also include the responsibility to remove 
Shrubs within the abutting Right-of-way. Whenever the Community Services Division or the 
City Forester determines that any such Tree or Shrub endangers, hinders or obstructs the use 
of the Right-of-way, is a Nuisance, or in any other way endangers the public health, safety and 
welfare, the Community Services Division shall issue a notice of violation to order the 
Maintenance of the Tree or Shrub by the Owner. (See Sections 7.16.205 and 7.16.208.)
B.City Assistance. An Abutting Owner may contact the City Forester to request the City’s 
assistance with the removal of a diseased, hazardous, or dead Tree in the Right-of-way. 
Diseased Trees are those diagnosed by the City Forester as having a highly contagious 
disease that could have catastrophic effects upon the City’s Trees. An Abutting Owner may 
also request the City’s assistance with the removal of storm damage from Right-of-way Trees 
to ensure that the Right-of-way is kept clear for vehicular and pedestrian traffic and that 
public safety, health and welfare is maintained.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §9, 2014; TP-2032 §9, 97; TP-1289 §12, 85)
7.16.205 Notice of Violation; Hearing; Notice to Appear.
 
A. A notice of violation issued pursuant to Section 7.16.190 B. or Section 7.16.200 A. shall be 
sent to the Owner, occupant or agent by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by 
personal service. If the property is unoccupied and the Owner is a nonresident of Kansas, the 
notice of violation shall be sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the last known 
address of the Owner.
B. The notice of violation shall state:
1. The common or legal description of the property, or both;
2. That the property is in violation of this Chapter;
3. A description of the violation that would reasonably allow the Owner, occupant or agent 
to determine the nature of the violation to allow for self-abatement;
4. That the violation must be abated by the Owner, occupant or agent within 10 days from 
the date of the notice of violation, provided that extensions of the time period shall be

granted if the Owner, occupant or agent demonstrates that due diligence is being 
exercised in abating the violation;
5. That prior to the expiration of the notice period, the Owner, occupant or agent may 
request in writing a hearing before the hearing officer;
6. That failure to comply with the notice of violation shall result in the City abating the 
violation, with the assessment of costs (including a reasonable administrative fee and the 
cost of all notice) made against the property and the Owner, occupant or agent;
7. That failure to pay such assessments within 30 days of the notice of costs shall result in 
the filing of a tax lien against the property or by filing for a personal judgment against the 
recipient, or both; and
8. That separate and independent of any abatement of the violation by the City, such 
violation is subject to prosecution in the Overland Park Municipal Court.
C. If the recipient of the notice of violation makes a written request for hearing within the notice 
period, then the City shall immediate schedule a hearing before a designated hearing officer. 
The hearing officer shall receive evidence, review the investigation, and prepare a written 
order. The order shall be sent to all relevant parties within 10 days of the hearing, unless 
otherwise stated at the hearing and prior to the City taking any action to abate the violation. 
The order shall state the relevant facts and the specific Code provisions relied upon, and any 
other stipulations, methods of abatement, or orders as deemed necessary by the hearing 
officer.
D. If an authorized public officer determines that a violation of this Chapter exists, the public 
officer may issue a Notice to Appear in Municipal Court for such violation. Such prosecution 
shall be separate and independent of any abatement action, and no other procedures are 
required as a prerequisite to the issuance of a Notice to Appear.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §10, 2014)
7.16.208 Abatement by City; Notice of Costs; Assessment and Collection.
 
A. If the Owner, occupant or agent fails to request a hearing and to self-abate the violation 
within the notice period, then the City may go onto the property to abate the violation in a 
reasonable manner. The City shall not be responsible for damage to property due to 
reasonable methods of gaining entrance onto the property or for damages to property in the 
reasonable exercise of its duty to abate the violation. The City may use its own employees or 
contract for services to abate the violation.
B. If the City abates the violation, it shall provide a notice of costs to the Owner, occupant or 
agent by certified mail, return receipt requested, at the last known mailing address. If the 
property is vacant or unoccupied, the notice of costs shall also be posted on the property in a 
reasonable manner. The notice of costs shall state:
1. The common or legal description of the property, or both;
2. The nature of the violation, including relevant ordinances;
3. The nature of the work performed to abate the violation;
4. The costs incurred for the abatement in either a lump sum or in itemized form;
5. That the notice of costs is a demand for payment within 30 days from the date of the 
notice;

7.16.210 Penalties and Violation; Civil Actions.
 
A. It shall be unlawful for any person to plant or otherwise maintain Trees or Shrubs in an area 
where such planting or Maintenance is prohibited by this Chapter.
B. It shall be unlawful for any person, including an Abutting Owner, to abuse, damage, mutilate, 
destroy or remove without the prior written permission from the City Forester (i) any Street 
Tree, (ii) any Shrub in the Right-of-way that was planted or is maintained by the City, or (iii) 
any Tree or Shrub on any other public property. Persons in violation of this provision shall be 
required to provide a replacement Tree or Shrub of a species and caliper approved by the 
City Forester; provided, however, the City Forester may waive this requirement in the City 
Forester’s sole discretion.
C. It shall be unlawful for any Owner, occupant or agent to permit any Tree or Shrub on their 
property to remain in violation of this Chapter after receipt of a notice of violation from the 
Community Services Division or the City Forester.
D. It shall be unlawful for any Owner, occupant or agent to allow any Tree or Shrub to hinder or 
obstruct any Right-of-way abutting their property or to violate any minimum distance or 
clearance requirements.
E. It shall be unlawful for any person to prevent, hinder or otherwise interfere with authorized 
employees, officials or contractors of the City in the performance of their official duties 
pursuant to the provisions of this Chapter.
F. Any person who violates any of the provisions of this Chapter is guilty of a public offense and 
upon conviction shall be punished as provided in OPMC Section 1.12.010.

7.16.220 Exceptions and Other Regulations.
 
A. Sections 7.16.150 A., 7.16.150 F. and 7.16.180 shall not affect Trees or Shrubs planted prior 
to the earliest effective date of the applicable prohibitions contained therein. The judgment of 
the City Forester concerning the planting date of the object at issue shall be entitled to a 
presumption of correctness that may be rebutted by credible evidence. Any replacement or 
additional plantings after such earliest effective date shall be in full compliance with all 
applicable provisions of the Overland Park Municipal Code.
B. The provisions contained in this Chapter are supplementary to and are not intended to 
exempt or interfere with the duties, responsibilities, enforcement or interpretation of any 
other ordinance or law concerning property maintenance, Right-of-way or the removal of 
vegetation, including, but not limited to, weeds, woody vines, brush, grass and uncultivated 
plants listed in Chapter 7.20 of the Overland Park Municipal Code.
(History: Ord. TP-3025 §13, 2014; TP-2032 §11, 97; TP-1289 §14, 85)
