2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3907.
 
Insufficiency	of	bond.
	If	a	supersedeas	bond	was	not	filed	with	the	notice	of
appeal,	and	if	the	action	is	not	yet	docketed	on	appeal,	a	bond	may	be	filed	with	the
court	from	which	the	appeal	is	taken.	After	the	action	is	so	docketed,	application	for
leave	to	file	a	bond	may	be	made	only	in	the	appellate	court.	When	the	surety	for	a
supersedeas	bond	shall	be	insufficient,	or	such	bond	is	insufficient	in	form	or	amount,
the	appellate	court	may	on	motion	order	a	change	or	renewal	of	such	bond,	and	direct
that	it	be	filed	in	such	court.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	92;	January	1,	2001.
