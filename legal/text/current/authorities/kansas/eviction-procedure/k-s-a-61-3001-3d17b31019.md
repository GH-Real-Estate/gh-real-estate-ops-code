2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3001.
 
Summons;	issuance.
	Upon	the	filing	of	the	petition	pursuant	to	the	code	of	civil
procedure	for	limited	actions,	the	clerk	of	the	district	court	shall	issue	a	summons	for
service	upon	each	defendant	in	accordance	with	this	act.	Additional	summonses	may	be
issued	as	requested.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	19;	January	1,	2001.
