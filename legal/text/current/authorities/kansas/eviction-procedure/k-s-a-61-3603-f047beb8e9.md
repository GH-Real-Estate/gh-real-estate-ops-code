2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3603.
 
Exemption	from	seizure	and	sale.
	The	provisions	of	article	23	of	chapter	60	of	the
Kansas	Statutes	Annotated,	and	amendments	thereto,	relating	to	exemptions	from
seizure	and	sale,	shall	apply	to	attachments,	executions	and	other	process	issued	from
any	court	in	this	state	pursuant	to	the	code	of	civil	procedure	for	limited	actions.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	64;	January	1,	2001.
