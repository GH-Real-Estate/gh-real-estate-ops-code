2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3904.
 
Execution	on	judgment	pending	appeal	without	bond.
	No	execution	shall	issue	upon	a
judgment,	nor	shall	proceedings	be	taken	for	its	enforcement,	until	the	expiration	of	14
days	after	its	entry.	If	an	appellant	does	not	file	a	supersedeas	bond	as	provided	in	the
code	of	civil	procedure	for	limited	actions,	the	taking	of	an	appeal	shall	not	operate	to
stay	proceedings	for	the	enforcement	of	a	final	judgment	or	to	take	execution	thereon.
Nothing	in	this	section	shall	be	construed	as	limiting	any	power	of	a	judge	hearing	such
appeal	to	stay	proceedings	during	the	pendency	of	an	appeal,	to	grant	an	injunction
during	the	pendency	of	such	appeal	or	to	make	any	other	appropriate	order	to	preserve
the	status	quo	or	the	effectiveness	of	the	judgment	subsequently	to	be	rendered.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	89;	L.	2010,	ch.	135,	§	222;	July	1.
