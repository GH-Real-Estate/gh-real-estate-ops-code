## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3802.
 
Judgment	not	bar	to	other	actions.
	A	judgment	in	a	lawsuit	brought	under	K.S.A.	61-
3801	through	61-3808,	and	amendments	thereto,	shall	not	be	a	bar	to	any	subsequent
lawsuit	brought	by	either	party	for	claims	not	included	in	such	judgment.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	79;	January	1,	2001.
