2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-4104.
 
Effect	on	pending	actions.
	The	procedures	prescribed	by	the	code	of	civil
procedure	for	limited	actions	shall	apply	to	any	actions	or	proceedings	commenced,	or
judgments	entered,	prior	to	the	enactment	of	this	act.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	100;	January	1,	2001.
