## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-2804.
 
Filings.
	Judicial	districts	in	this	state	may	accept	for	filing	under	this	act	lawsuits
filed	in	the	same	method	in	which	lawsuits	are	filed	prior	to	the	adoption	of	this	act,	or
filed	pursuant	to	an	electronic	filing	procedure,	or	a	combination	of	the	two,	as	long	as
any	such	filings	comply	with	the	rules	of	the	supreme	court	of	this	state.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	4;	January	1,	2001.
