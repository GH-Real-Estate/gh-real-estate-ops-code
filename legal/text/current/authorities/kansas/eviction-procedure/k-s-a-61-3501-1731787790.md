2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3501.
 
Attachment.
	The	provisions	of	article	7	of	chapter	60	of	the	Kansas	Statutes
Annotated,	and	amendments	thereto,	relating	to	attachment	shall	govern	attachment
proceedings	for	actions	pursuant	to	the	code	of	civil	procedure	for	limited	actions,
except	the	provisions	of	K.S.A.	60-711,	and	amendments	thereto,	relating	to	the
appointment	of	a	receiver,	and	the	provisions	of	article	7	of	chapter	60	of	the	Kansas
Statutes	Annotated	relating	to	the	attachment	of	real	property,	shall	not	be	applicable	in
lawsuits	filed	under	the	code	of	civil	procedure	for	limited	actions.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	46;	January	1,	2001.
