2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3102.
 
Expenses	on	refusal	to	admit	truth	of	matter	requested.
	If	a	party	to	whom	a	request
for	admission	has	been	submitted	denies	under	oath	any	matter	requested,	and	the
party	submitting	the	request	later	proves	the	genuineness	of	any	document	or	the	truth
of	any	matter	of	fact	denied	by	the	answering	party,	the	party	submitting	the	request
may	ask	the	court	for	an	order	requiring	the	answering	party	to	pay	the	reasonable
expenses	incurred	in	making	such	proof,	including	reasonable	attorney	fees.	The	court
shall	enter	the	order	unless	the	court	finds	that	there	were	good	reasons	for	the	denial
or	that	the	admissions	sought	were	of	no	substantial	importance.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	26;	January	1,	2001.
