2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3906.
 
Forcible	detainer.
	In	appeals	taken	by	the	defendant	in	actions	for	the	forcible
detention	of	real	property,	the	supersedeas	bond	filed	on	appeal	shall	be	conditioned
that	the	appellant	will	not	commit	or	suffer	waste	to	be	committed	on	the	premises	in
controversy,	and	if	upon	appeal	the	court	judgment	be	rendered	against	the	appellant,
the	appellant	will	pay	the	value	of	the	use	and	occupation	of	the	property,	from	the	date
such	bond	was	filed	until	the	delivery	of	the	property	pursuant	to	the	judgment,	and	all
damages	and	costs	that	may	be	awarded	against	the	appellant.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	91;	January	1,	2001.
