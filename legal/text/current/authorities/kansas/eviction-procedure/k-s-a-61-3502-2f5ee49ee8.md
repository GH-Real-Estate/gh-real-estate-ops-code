2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3502.
 
Nature	of	garnishment.
	Garnishment	is	a	procedure	whereby	the	wages,	money	or
intangible	property	of	a	person	can	be	seized	or	attached	pursuant	to	an	order	of
garnishment	issued	by	the	court	under	the	conditions	set	forth	in	the	order.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	47;	January	1,	2001.
