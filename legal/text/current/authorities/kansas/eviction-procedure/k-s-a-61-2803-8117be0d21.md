2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-2803.
 
Supreme	court	rules.
	The	supreme	court	of	this	state	shall	adopt	rules	to	govern
the	electronic	filing	of	court	matters	and	the	storage	of	and	access	by	the	public	to	the
same	and	such	other	matters	as	is	necessary	under	the	code	of	civil	procedure	for
limited	actions.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	3;	L.	2002,	ch.	157,	§	2;	July	1.
