2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3405.
 
Actions	against	public	utility,	common	carrier	or	transportation	system.
	Any	action
brought	against	a	public	utility,	common	carrier	or	transportation	system	for	any
liability	or	penalty	or	forfeiture,	may	be	brought	in	any	county	into	or	through	which
such	public	utility,	common	carrier	or	transportation	system	operates	regularly.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	41;	January	1,	2001.
