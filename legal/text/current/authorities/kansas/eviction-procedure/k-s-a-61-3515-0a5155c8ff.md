2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3515.
 
Failure	of	garnishee	to	pay.
	If	after	the	time	the	garnishee	is	to	make	payment	of
funds	or	property	held	under	a	garnishment,	the	garnishee	fails	or	refuses	to	pay	or
deliver	property	to	the	judgment	creditor,	the	judgment	creditor	may	file	a	motion	and
shall	send	a	copy	of	the	motion	to	the	garnishee	and	the	judgment	debtor	in	the	manner
allowed	under	K.S.A.	61-2907,	and	amendments	thereto.	At	the	hearing	on	the	motion,
the	court	may	find	the	garnishee	in	contempt	and	punish	the	garnishee	by	a	fine	or	may
enter	judgment	against	the	garnishee	for	such	amount	as	the	court	deems	reasonable
and	proper,	including	the	expenses	and	attorney	fees	of	the	judgment	creditor.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	60;	January	1,	2001.
