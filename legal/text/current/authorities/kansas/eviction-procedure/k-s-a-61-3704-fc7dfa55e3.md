61-3704.
 
Return;	confirmation	of	sale.
	Upon	sale	of	such	property,	the	appropriate	officer
shall	make	a	return	of	such	sale	to	the	clerk	of	the	court,	together	with	an	itemization	of
the	officer's	expenses	of	sale.	The	court,	upon	finding	the	proceedings	regular	and	in
conformity	with	law,	shall	confirm	the	same,	and	order	the	officer	to	make	to	the
purchaser	a	certificate	of	sale.	The	certificate	of	sale	shall	accurately	describe	the
property	sold,	name	the	purchaser	and	recite	the	facts	of	the	sale.	If	a	certificate	of	title
is	obtainable	for	such	property	under	the	laws	of	this	state,	the	description	of	the
property	shall	include	the	year,	make,	style	and	identification	number	of	such	property.
A	certificate	of	sale	shall	vest	title	to	the	property	in	the	purchaser.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	76;	January	1,	2001.
