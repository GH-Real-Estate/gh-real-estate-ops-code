2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-4101.
 
Lost	or	destroyed	court	files.
	The	applicable	provisions	of	article	25	of	chapter	60	of
the	Kansas	Statutes	Annotated,	and	amendments	thereto,	relating	to	lost	or	destroyed
court	files	and	records,	shall	govern	lost	court	files	and	records	in	actions	pursuant	to
the	code	of	civil	procedure	for	limited	actions,	except	as	otherwise	provided	in	the	rules
of	the	supreme	court	of	this	state.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	97;	January	1,	2001.
