2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3408.
 
Time	for	objection	to	venue.
	Objection	to	the	venue	of	an	action	shall	not	be
allowed	except	on	timely	motion	made	and	for	grounds	established	before	trial	of	the
action	is	commenced	on	the	merits.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	44;	January	1,	2001.
