2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3806.
 
Appearance;	answer.
	A	defendant	shall	either	appear	in	person	or	by	counsel	at
the	time	and	date	set	forth	in	the	summons	or	file	on	or	before	such	date	a	written
answer.	The	answer,	when	filed,	shall	contain	the	information	as	required	under
subsection	(b)	of	K.S.A.	61-2904,	and	amendments	thereto.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	83;	January	1,	2001.
