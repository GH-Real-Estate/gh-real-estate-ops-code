2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3503.
 
When	garnishment	available	before	judgment.
	An	order	of	garnishment	before
judgment	may	be	obtained	only	upon	order	of	a	judge	of	the	district	court	pursuant	to
the	procedure	to	obtain	an	order	of	attachment.	No	order	of	garnishment	may	be
obtained	before	judgment	where	the	property	sought	to	be	attached	is	wages	earned	by
the	person	being	garnished.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	48;	January	1,	2001.
