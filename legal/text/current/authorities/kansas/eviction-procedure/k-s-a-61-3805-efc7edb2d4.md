2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3805.
 
Summons;	time	for	appearance.
	The	time	stated	in	the	summons	requiring	the
defendant	to	appear	in	response	to	the	petition	shall	be	determined	by	the	court.	Such
time	shall	be	not	less	than	three	nor	more	than	14	days	after	the	date	the	summons	is
issued.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	82;	L.	2002,	ch.	157,	§	18;	July	1.
