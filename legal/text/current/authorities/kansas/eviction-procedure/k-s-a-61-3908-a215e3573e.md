2025	Kansas	Statutes
61-3908.
 
Judgment	against	surety.
	By	entering	into	a	supersedeas	bond	given	pursuant	to
K.S.A.	61-3905	and	61-3906,	and	amendments	thereto,	the	surety	submits	to	the
jurisdiction	of	the	court	wherein	the	judgment	becomes	final,	and	irrevocably	appoints
the	clerk	of	such	court	as	the	surety's	agent	upon	whom	any	papers	affecting	the
surety's	liability	on	the	bond	may	be	served.	The	surety's	liability	may	be	enforced	on
motion	without	the	necessity	of	an	independent	action.	The	motion	and	such	notice	of
the	motion	as	the	judge	prescribes	may	be	served	on	the	clerk	of	the	court	who	shall
forthwith	mail	copies	to	the	surety	if	the	surety's	address	is	known.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	161,	§	93;	January	1,	2001.
