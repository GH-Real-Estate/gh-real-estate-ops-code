2025	Kansas	Statutes
39-1107.
 Rights	of	persons	with	disabilities;	use	of	hearing	assistance	dog	by	person	who	is	deaf	or
hard	of	hearing.
	Every	person	who	is	deaf	or	hard	of	hearing	has	the	right	to	be
accompanied	by	a	hearing	assistance	dog,	specially	selected,	trained	and	tested	for	the
purpose	of	hearing	assistance,	in	or	upon	any	of	the	places	listed	in	K.S.A.	39-1101,	and
amendments	thereto,	in	the	acquisition	and	use	of	rental,	residential	housing	and	in	the
purchase	and	use	of	residential	housing	without	being	required	to	pay	an	extra	charge
for	the	hearing	assistance	dog.	The	person	who	is	deaf	or	hard	of	hearing	shall	be	liable
for	any	damage	done	to	the	premises	or	facilities	by	such	dog.
History: 
L.	1982,	ch.	184,	§	1;	L.	2003,	ch.	64,	§	5;	L.	2022,	ch.	75,	§	6;	April	28.
