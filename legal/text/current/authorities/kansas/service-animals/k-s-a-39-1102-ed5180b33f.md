2025	Kansas	Statutes
39-1102.
 
Same;	use	of	guide	dog	by	legally	blind	person.
	Every	legally	blind	person	shall	have
the	right	to	be	accompanied	by	a	guide	dog,	specially	selected,	trained	and	tested	for	the
purpose,	in	or	upon	any	of	the	places	listed	in	K.S.A.	39-1101,	and	amendments	thereto,
in	the	acquisition	and	use	of	rental,	residential	housing	and	in	the	purchase	and	use	of
residential	housing	without	being	required	to	pay	an	extra	charge	for	the	guide	dog,
except	that	such	person	shall	be	liable	for	any	damage	done	to	the	premises	or	facilities
by	such	dog.
History:
 L.	1969,	ch.	54,	§	2;	L.	1987,	ch.	155,	§	1;	L.	2003,	ch.	64,	§	3;	July	1.
