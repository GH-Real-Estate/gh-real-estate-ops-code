2025	Kansas	Statutes
39-1108.
 
Same;	use	of	service	dog	by	person	with	disability.
	Every	person	with	a	disability	shall
have	the	right	to	be	accompanied	by	a	service	dog,	specially	selected,	trained	and	tested
for	the	purpose	which	shall	include,	but	not	be	limited	to,	pulling	a	wheelchair,	opening
doors	and	picking	up	objects,	in	or	upon	any	of	the	places	listed	in	K.S.A.	39-1101,	and
amendments	thereto,	in	the	acquisition	and	use	of	rental,	residential	housing	and	in	the
purchase	and	use	of	residential	housing	without	being	required	to	pay	an	extra	charge
for	the	service	dog.	Such	person	shall	be	liable	for	any	damage	done	to	the	premises	by
such	dog.
History:
 L.	1989,	ch.	127,	§	1;	L.	1992,	ch.	205,	§	14;	L.	2003,	ch.	64,	§	6;	July	1.
