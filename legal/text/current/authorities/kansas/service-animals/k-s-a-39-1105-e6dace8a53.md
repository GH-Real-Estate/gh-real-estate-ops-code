2025	Kansas	Statutes
39-1105.
 
Same;	employment.
	It	is	the	policy	of	this	state	that	the	blind,	the	visually
handicapped	and	persons	who	are	otherwise	physically	disabled	shall	be	employed	in
the	service	of	the	state,	political	subdivisions	of	the	state,	the	public	schools	and	in	all
other	employment	supported	in	whole	or	in	part	by	public	funds,	on	the	same	terms	and
conditions	as	the	able-bodied,	unless	it	is	shown	that	the	particular	disability	prevents
the	performance	of	the	work	involved.
History:
 L.	1969,	ch.	54,	§	6;	July	1.
