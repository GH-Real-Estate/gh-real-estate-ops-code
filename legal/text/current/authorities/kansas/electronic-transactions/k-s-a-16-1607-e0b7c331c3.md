## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
16-1607.
 
Legal	recognition	of	electronic	records,	electronic	signatures	and	electronic	contracts.
	(a)
A	record	or	signature	may	not	be	denied	legal	effect	or	enforceability	solely	because	it	is
in	electronic	form.
(b) A	contract	may	not	be	denied	legal	effect	or	enforceability	solely	because	an
electronic	record	was	used	in	its	formation.
(c) If	a	law	requires	a	record	to	be	in	writing,	an	electronic	record	satisfies	the	law.
(d) If	a	law	requires	a	signature,	an	electronic	signature	satisfies	the	law.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	120,	§	7;	July	1.
