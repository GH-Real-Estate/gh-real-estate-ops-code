2025	Kansas	Statutes
16-1606.
 
Construction	and	application.
	This	act	must	be	construed	and	applied:
(a) To	facilitate	electronic	transactions	consistent	with	other	applicable	law;
(b) to	be	consistent	with	reasonable	practices	concerning	electronic	transactions	and
with	the	continued	expansion	of	those	practices;	and
(c) to	effectuate	its	general	purpose	to	make	uniform	the	law	with	respect	to	the
subject	of	this	act	among	states	enacting	it.
History:
 L.	2000,	ch.	120,	§	6;	July	1.
