2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2549.
 
Receipt	of	rent	subject	to	certain	obligations.
	A	rental	agreement,	assignment,
conveyance,	trust	deed	or	security	instrument	may	not	permit	the	receipt	of	rent	free	of
the	obligation	to	comply	with	subsection	(a)	of	K.S.A.	58-2553.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	290,	§	10;	July	1.
