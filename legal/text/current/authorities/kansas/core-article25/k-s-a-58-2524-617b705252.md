2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2524.
 
Rent	as	lien	on	crop.
	Any	rent	due	for	farming	land	shall	be	a	lien	on	the	crop
growing	or	made	on	the	premises.	Such	lien	may	be	enforced	by	action	and	attachment
therein,	as	hereinafter	provided.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	24;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-524.
