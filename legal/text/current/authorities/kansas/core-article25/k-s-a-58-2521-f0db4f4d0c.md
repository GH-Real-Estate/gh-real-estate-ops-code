## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2521.
 
Repairs	and	improvements	by	cotenant.
	If	a	joint	tenant,	or	tenant	in	common,	or
tenant	in	coparcenary,	have	by	consent	management	of	the	estate,	and	make	repairs
and	improvements	with	the	knowledge	and	without	objection	of	his	or	her	cotenant	or
coparcener,	such	cotenant	or	coparcener	shall	contribute	ratably	thereto.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	21;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-521.
