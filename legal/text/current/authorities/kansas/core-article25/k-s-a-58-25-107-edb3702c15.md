2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-25,107.
 
Receipt	of	rent	subject	to	certain	obligations.
	A	rental	agreement,	assignment,
conveyance,	trust	deed	or	security	instrument	shall	not	permit	the	receipt	of	rent,	unless
the	landlord	has	agreed	to	comply	with	K.S.A.	58-25,111.
