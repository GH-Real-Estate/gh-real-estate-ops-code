2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2522.
 
Recovery	by	cotenant	of	rents	and	profits.
	A	joint	tenant,	or	tenant	in	common,	or
tenant	in	coparcenary,	may	maintain	an	action	against	his	or	her	cotenant	or
coparcener	or	their	personal	representatives,	for	receiving	more	than	his	or	her	just
proportion	of	the	rents	and	profits.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	22;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-522.
