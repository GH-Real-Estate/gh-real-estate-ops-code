## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2513.
 
Attornment	of	tenant	unnecessary;	payment	of	rent.
	A	conveyance	of	real	estate	or	of
any	interest	therein	by	a	landlord	shall	be	valid	without	the	attornment	of	the	tenant;
but	the	payment	of	rent	by	the	tenant	to	the	grantor	at	any	time	before	notice	of	sale
given	to	said	tenant	shall	be	good	against	the	grantee.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	13;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-513.
