2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-25,110.
 
Delivery	of	possession	of	mobile	home	space;	action	for	possession;	damages.
	At	the
commencement	of	the	term	the	landlord	shall	deliver	possession	of	the	mobile	home
space	to	the	tenant	in	compliance	with	the	rental	agreement	and	K.S.A.	58-25,111.	The
landlord	may	bring	an	action	for	possession	against	any	person	wrongfully	in
possession	and	may	recover	the	damages	provided	in	K.S.A.	58-25,123.
History:
 L.	1992,	ch.	306,	§	12;	July	1.
