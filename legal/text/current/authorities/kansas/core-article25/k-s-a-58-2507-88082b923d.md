2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2507.
 
Termination	of	lease	for	three	months	or	longer;	notice;	effect	of	payment	of	rent.
	If	a
tenant	for	a	period	of	three	months	or	longer	neglect	or	refuse	to	pay	rent	when	due,	ten
days'	notice	in	writing	to	quit	shall	determine	the	lease,	unless	such	rent	be	paid	before
the	expiration	of	said	ten	days.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	7;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-507.
