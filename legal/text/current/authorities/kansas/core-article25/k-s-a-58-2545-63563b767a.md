2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2545.
 
Rental	agreement;	terms	and	conditions	in	absence	thereof.
	(a)	The	landlord	and	tenant
may	include	in	a	rental	agreement,	terms	and	conditions	not	prohibited	by	this	act	or
other	rule	of	law,	including	rent,	term	of	the	agreement	and	other	provisions	governing
the	rights	and	obligations	of	the	parties.
(b) In	absence	of	agreement,	the	tenant	shall	pay	as	rent	the	fair	rental	value	for	the
use	and	occupancy	of	the	dwelling	unit.
(c) Rent	shall	be	payable	without	demand	or	notice	at	the	time	and	place	agreed	upon
by	the	parties.	Unless	otherwise	agreed,	rent	is	payable	at	the	dwelling	unit,	and
periodic	rent	is	payable	at	the	beginning	of	any	term	of	one	(1)	month	or	less	and
otherwise	in	equal	monthly	installments	at	the	beginning	of	each	month.	Unless
otherwise	agreed,	rent	shall	be	uniformly	apportionable	from	day-to-day.
(d) Unless	the	rental	agreement	fixes	a	definite	term,	the	tenancy	shall	be	week-to-
week,	in	the	case	of	a	roomer	who	pays	weekly	rent,	and	in	all	other	cases	month-to-
month.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	290,	§	6;	July	1.
