2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2567.
 
Lien	or	security	interest	in	tenant's	personal	property	unenforceable;	distraint	abolished,
exception.
	(a)	Except	as	otherwise	provided	in	this	act,	a	lien	or	security	interest	on
behalf	of	the	landlord	in	the	tenant's	household	goods,	furnishings,	fixtures	or	other
personal	property	is	not	enforceable	unless	perfected	prior	to	the	effective	date	of	this
act.
(b) Except	as	otherwise	provided	in	K.S.A.	58-2565,	distraint	for	rent	is	abolished.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	290,	§	28;	July	1.
