2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2516.
 
Remedies	of	alienees	of	lessors	and	lessees.
	Alienees	of	lessors	and	lessees	of	land
shall	have	the	same	legal	remedies	in	relation	to	such	land	as	their	principal.
