2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2512.
 
Re-entry	of	premises	upon	violation	of	K.S.A.	58-2511.
	If	any	tenant	shall	violate	the
provisions	of	the	preceding	section,	the	landlord	or	person	holding	under	the	landlord,
after	giving	ten	days'	notice	to	quit	possession,	shall	have	a	right	to	re-enter	the
premises	and	take	possession	thereof	and	dispossess	the	tenant,	subtenant	or
undertenant.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	12;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-512.
