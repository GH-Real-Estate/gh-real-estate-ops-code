2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-25,124.
 
Tenant's	refusal	to	allow	lawful	access,	remedies;	landlord's	unlawful	or	unreasonable
entry,	remedies.
	(a)	If	the	tenant	refuses	to	allow	lawful	access	to	the	mobile	home	space,
the	landlord	may	obtain	injunctive	relief	to	compel	access	or	may	terminate	the	rental
agreement.	In	either	case,	the	landlord	may	recover	actual	damages.
(b) If	the	landlord	makes	an	unlawful	entry	or	a	lawful	entry	to	the	mobile	home	space
in	an	unreasonable	manner	or	makes	repeated	demands	for	entry	otherwise	lawful	but
which	have	the	effect	of	unreasonably	harassing	the	tenant,	the	tenant	may	obtain
injunctive	relief	to	prevent	the	recurrence	of	the	conduct	or	terminate	the	rental
agreement.	In	either	case,	the	tenant	may	recover	actual	damages.
