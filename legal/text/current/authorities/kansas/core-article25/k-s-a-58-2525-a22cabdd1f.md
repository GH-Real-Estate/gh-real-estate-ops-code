2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2525.
 
Lessor's	remedies	when	rent	payable	in	share	of	crop.
	When	any	such	rent	is	payable
in	a	share	or	certain	proportion	of	the	crop,	the	lessor	shall	be	deemed	the	owner	of
such	share	or	proportion,	and	may,	if	the	tenant	refuse	to	deliver	the	lessor	such	share
or	proportion,	enter	upon	the	land	and	take	possession	of	the	same,	or	obtain	possession
thereof	by	action	of	replevin.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	25;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-525.
