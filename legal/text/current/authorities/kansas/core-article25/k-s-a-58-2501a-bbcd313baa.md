## Official source page 1

2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2501a.
 
When	tenant	may	remove	buildings	and	improvements.
	Where	the	tenant	in
possession	of	farm	lands	under	lease,	with	the	owner	as	provided	in	K.S.A.	58-2501	owns
substantially	all	the	improvements	on	the	land,	the	tenant	may	transfer	his	or	her	term
and	improvements	without	the	consent	of	the	landlord,	and	any	provisions	in	the	lease
prohibiting	such	transfer	or	requiring	the	tenant	or	the	tenant's	assignee	to	remove	such
buildings	or	improvements,	that	does	not	require	the	landlord	or	the	new	tenant	to	pay
the	owner	thereof	the	fair	value	of	the	improvements	to	the	land	at	the	time	of	the
expiration	of	the	lease,	shall	be	void.
History:
 L.	1925,	ch.	208,	§	2;	May	28.
