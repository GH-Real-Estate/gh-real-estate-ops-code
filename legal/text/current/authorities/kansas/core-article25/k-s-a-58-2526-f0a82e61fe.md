2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2526.
 
Recovery	of	rent	from	purchaser	of	crop.
	The	person	entitled	to	the	rent	may	recover
from	the	purchaser	of	the	crop,	or	any	part	thereof,	with	notice	of	the	lien	the	value	of
the	crop	purchased,	to	the	extent	of	the	rent	due	and	damages.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	26;	October	31;	R.S.	1923,	67-526.
