2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2508.
 
Termination	of	tenancy	of	less	than	three	months	for	nonpayment	of	rent;	notice.
	If	a
tenant	for	a	period	of	less	than	three	months	shall	neglect	or	refuse	to	pay	rent	when
due,	three	days'	notice	in	writing	to	quit	shall	determine	the	same,	unless	such	rent	be
paid	before	the	expiration	of	said	three	days.
History:
 G.S.	1868,	ch.	55,	§	8;	L.	1905,	ch.	280,	§	1;	March	4;	R.S.	1923,	67-508.
