2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2519.
 
Executors	and	administrators,	remedies	and	liabilities.
	Executors	and	administrators
shall	have	the	same	remedies	to	recover	rents,	and	be	subject	to	the	same	liabilities	to
pay	them,	as	their	testators	and	intestates.
