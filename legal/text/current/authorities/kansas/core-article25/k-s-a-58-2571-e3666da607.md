2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2571.
 
Tenant's	refusal	to	allow	lawful	access;	remedies;	landlord's	unlawful	or	unreasonable
entry;	remedies.
	(a)	If	the	tenant	refuses	to	allow	lawful	access	to	the	dwelling	unit,	the
landlord	may	obtain	injunctive	relief	to	compel	access,	or	may	terminate	the	rental
agreement.	In	either	case,	the	landlord	may	recover	actual	damages.
(b) If	the	landlord	makes	an	unlawful	entry,	or	a	lawful	entry	in	an	unreasonable
manner,	or	makes	repeated	demands	for	entry	otherwise	lawful	but	which	have	the
effect	of	unreasonably	harassing	the	tenant,	the	tenant	may	obtain	injunctive	relief	to
prevent	the	recurrence	of	the	conduct,	or	may	terminate	the	rental	agreement.	In	either
case,	the	tenant	may	recover	actual	damages.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	290,	§	32;	July	1.
