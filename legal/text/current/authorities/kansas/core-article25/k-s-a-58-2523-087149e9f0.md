2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2523.
 
Action	by	remainderman	or	reversioner	for	waste	or	trespass.
	A	person	seized	of	an
estate	in	remainder	or	reversion	may	maintain	an	action	for	waste	or	trespass	for	injury
to	the	inheritance,	notwithstanding	an	intervening	estate	for	life	or	years.
