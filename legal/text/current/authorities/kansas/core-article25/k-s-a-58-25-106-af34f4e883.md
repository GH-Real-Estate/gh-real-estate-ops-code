2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-25,106.
 
Prohibited	terms	and	conditions.
	(a)	A	rental	agreement	shall	not	provide	that	the
tenant	or	landlord	does	any	of	the	following:
(1) Agrees	to	waive	or	to	forego	rights	or	remedies	under	this	act;
(2) authorizes	any	person	to	confess	judgment	on	a	claim	arising	out	of	the	rental
agreement;
(3) agrees	to	pay	the	other	party's	attorney	fees;
(4) agrees	to	the	exculpation	or	limitation	of	any	liability	of	the	other	party	arising
under	law	or	to	indemnify	the	other	party	for	that	liability	or	the	costs	connected
therewith;	or
(5) agrees	to	a	designated	agent	for	the	sale	of	tenant's	mobile	home.
(b) A	provision	prohibited	by	subsection	(a)	included	in	a	rental	agreement	is
unenforceable.
History:
 L.	1992,	ch.	306,	§	8;	July	1.
