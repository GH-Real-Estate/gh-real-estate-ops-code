2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2558.
 
Use	of	premises;	extended	absence	of	tenant.
	Unless	otherwise	agreed,	the	tenant
shall	occupy	such	tenant's	dwelling	unit	only	as	a	dwelling	unit.	The	rental	agreement
may	require	that	the	tenant	notify	the	landlord	of	any	anticipated	extended	absence
from	the	premises	in	excess	of	seven	days	no	later	than	the	first	day	of	the	extended
absence.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	290,	§	19;	July	1.
