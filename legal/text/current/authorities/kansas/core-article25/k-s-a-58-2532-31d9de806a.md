2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-2532.
 
Terms	of	such	contracts	variant	from	approved	rental	agreements.
	The	foregoing	lease
conditions	and	requirements	are	variant	from	the	ordinary	and	generally	used	and
approved	rental	agreements	between	landlord	and	tenant	in	the	particulars	above
mentioned,	are	harsh,	burdensome,	oppressive	and	extortionate	in	their	terms,	and	are
entered	into	by	necessitous	persons	only	because	of	the	scarcity	of	other	rental	lands.
On	account	of	the	pledge	of	lien	by	the	tenant	to	the	landlord	of	the	tenant's	crops,
teams,	and	all	his	or	her	other	property	the	tenant	is	deprived	of	credit	with	merchants
and	banks	for	the	purchase	of	the	comforts	and	conveniences	of	ordinary	farm	life,	his
or	her	children	deprived	of	educational	advantages,	and	himself	or	herself	and	family
kept	impoverished	in	condition	and	estate.
History:
 L.	1933,	ch.	233,	§	2;	June	5.
