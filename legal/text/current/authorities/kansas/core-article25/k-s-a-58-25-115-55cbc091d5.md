2025	Kansas	Statutes
58-25,115.
 
Landlord's	right	to	access;	limitations.
	(a)	A	landlord	shall	not	have	the	right	of
access	to	a	mobile	home	owned	by	a	tenant	without	consent	of	the	tenant	unless	it	is	an
extreme	hazard	involving	the	potential	loss	of	life	or	severe	property	damage.
(b) The	landlord	may	enter	onto	the	mobile	home	space	at	reasonable	hours	in	order
to	inspect	the	mobile	home	space,	make	necessary	or	agreed	repairs	or	improvements,
supply	necessary	or	agreed	services	or	exhibit	the	mobile	home	space	to	prospective	or
actual	purchasers,	mortgagees,	tenants,	workers	or	contractors.
(c) The	landlord	shall	not	abuse	the	right	of	access	or	use	it	to	harass	the	tenant.
