2025	Kansas	Statutes
31-132a.
 
Fire	safety	and	prevention;	apartment	house,	defined.
	Every	building	or	other
structure,	together	with	any	building	or	structure	used	in	connection	therewith,	kept,
used,	maintained,	advertised,	or	held	out	to	the	public	to	be	a	place	where	furnished	or
unfurnished	living	accommodations	other	than	sleeping	accommodations	for	transient
guests	may	be	rented	as	a	single	room	or	as	a	suite	of	rooms,	containing	three	(3)	or
more	single	units	or	suites,	or	both,	regardless	of	the	number	of	tenants	therein,	and
regardless	of	whether	any	such	room	or	suite	of	rooms	is	occupied	by	an	owner	or
operator	of	such	a	building	or	structure,	shall,	for	the	purpose	of	this	act,	be	deemed	an
apartment	house.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	220,	§	2;	July	1.
