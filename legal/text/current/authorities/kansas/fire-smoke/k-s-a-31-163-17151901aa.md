31-163.
 
Same;	violations;	penalties.
	Failure	to	place	or	maintain	a	smoke	detector	as
provided	by	the	smoke	detector	act	shall	be	a	nonclass	nonperson	misdemeanor.	Any
fine	imposed	for	a	violation	of	this	section	shall	not	exceed	$25.
