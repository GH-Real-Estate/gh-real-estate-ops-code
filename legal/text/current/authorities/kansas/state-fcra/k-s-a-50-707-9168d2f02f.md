2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-707.
 
Disclosures	to	governmental	agencies.
	Notwithstanding	the	provisions	of	K.S.A.	50-
703,	a	consumer	reporting	agency	may	furnish	identifying	information	respecting	any
consumer,	limited	to	name,	address,	former	addresses,	places	of	employment,	or	former
places	of	employment,	to	a	governmental	agency.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	85,	§	142;	January	1,	1974.
