2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-720.
 
Penalties.
	Any	person	violating	any	of	the	provisions	of	K.S.A.	50-701	to	50-719,
inclusive,	and	amendments	thereto,	for	which	penalties	are	not	otherwise	hereinbefore
provided	shall	be	deemed	guilty	of	a	class	C	misdemeanor	and	upon	conviction	thereof
shall	be	punished	in	the	manner	provided	by	law.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	85,	§	155;	January	1,	1974.
