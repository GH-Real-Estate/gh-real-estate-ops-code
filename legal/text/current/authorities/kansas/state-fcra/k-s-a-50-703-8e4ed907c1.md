2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-703.
 
Permissible	purposes	of	reports.
	A	consumer	reporting	agency	may	furnish	a
consumer	report	under	the	following	circumstances	and	no	other:
(a) In	response	to	the	order	of	a	court	having	jurisdiction	to	issue	such	an	order;
(b) in	accordance	with	the	written	instructions	of	the	consumer	to	whom	it	relates;	and
(c) to	a	person	which	it	has	reason	to	believe
(1) intends	to	use	the	information	in	connection	with	a	credit	transaction	involving	the
consumer	on	whom	the	information	is	to	be	furnished	and	involving	the	extension	of
credit	to,	or	review	or	collection	of	an	account	of,	the	consumer;	or
(2) intends	to	use	the	information	for	employment	purposes;	or
(3) intends	to	use	the	information	in	connection	with	the	underwriting	of	insurance
involving	the	consumer;	or
(4) intends	to	use	the	information	in	connection	with	a	determination	of	the
consumer's	eligibility	for	a	license	or	other	benefit	granted	by	a	governmental
instrumentality	required	by	law	to	consider	an	applicant's	financial	responsibility	or
status;	or
(5) otherwise	has	a	legitimate	business	need	for	the	information	in	connection	with	a
business	transaction	involving	the	consumer.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	85,	§	138;	January	1,	1974.
