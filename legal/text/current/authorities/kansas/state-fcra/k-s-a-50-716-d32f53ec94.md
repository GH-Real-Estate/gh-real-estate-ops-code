2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-716.
 
Civil	liability	for	negligent	noncompliance.
	Any	consumer	reporting	agency	or	user	of
information	which	is	negligent	in	failing	to	comply	with	any	requirement	imposed
under	K.S.A.	50-701	to	50-722,	inclusive,	and	amendments	thereto,	with	respect	to	any
consumer	is	liable	to	that	consumer	in	an	amount	equal	to	the	sum	of
(a) any	actual	damages	sustained	by	the	consumer	as	a	result	of	the	failure;
(b) in	the	case	of	any	successful	action	to	enforce	any	liability	under	this	section,	the
costs	of	the	action	together	with	reasonable	attorney's	fees	as	determined	by	the	court.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	85,	§	151;	January	1,	1974.
