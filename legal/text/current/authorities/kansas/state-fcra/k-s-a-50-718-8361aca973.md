2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-718.
 
Obtaining	information	under	false	pretenses.
	Any	person	who	knowingly	and	willfully
obtains	information	on	a	consumer	from	a	consumer	reporting	agency	under	false
pretenses	is	guilty	of	a	severity	level	7,	person	felony	and	upon	conviction	shall	be
punished	in	the	manner	provided	by	law.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	85,	§	153;	L.	2000,	ch.	181,	§	12;	July	1.
