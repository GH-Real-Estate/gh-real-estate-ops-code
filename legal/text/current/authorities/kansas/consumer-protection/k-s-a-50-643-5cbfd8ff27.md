2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-643.
 
Severability.
	If	any	provision	of	this	act	or	the	application	thereof	to	any	person
or	circumstances	is	held	invalid,	the	invalidity	does	not	affect	other	provisions	or
applications	of	this	act	which	can	be	given	effect	without	the	invalid	provision	or
application,	and	to	this	end	the	provisions	of	this	act	are	severable.
History:
 L.	1973,	ch.	217,	§	21;	January	1,	1974.
