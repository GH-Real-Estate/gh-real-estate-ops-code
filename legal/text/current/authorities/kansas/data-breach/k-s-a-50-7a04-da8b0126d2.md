2025	Kansas	Statutes
50-7a04.
 
Severability	clause.
	If	any	provision	of	this	act	or	its	application	to	any	person	or
circumstance	is	held	invalid,	the	invalidity	shall	not	affect	any	other	provision	or
application	of	the	act	which	can	be	given	effect	without	the	invalid	provision	or
application.	To	this	end	the	provisions	of	this	act	are	severable.
History:
 L.	2006,	ch.	149,	§	15;	July	1.
