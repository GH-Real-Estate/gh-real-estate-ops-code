2025	Kansas	Statutes
65-1,211.
 
Same;	additional	remedies.
	Notwithstanding	any	other	remedy	and	in	addition	to
any	other	remedy,	the	secretary	may	maintain,	in	the	manner	provided	by	the	Kansas
judicial	review	act,	an	action	in	the	name	of	the	state	of	Kansas	for	injunction	or	other
process	against	any	business	entity	or	individual	to	restrain	or	prevent	any	violation	of
the	provisions	of	the	residential	childhood	lead	poisoning	prevention	act	or	of	any	rules
and	regulations	adopted	under	the	residential	childhood	lead	poisoning	prevention	act.
History:
 L.	1999,	ch.	99,	§	13;	L.	2010,	ch.	17,	§	117;	July	1.
