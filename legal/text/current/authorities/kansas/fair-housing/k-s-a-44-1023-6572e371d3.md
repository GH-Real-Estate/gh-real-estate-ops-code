2025	Kansas	Statutes
44-1023.
 
Same;	assignment	of	case;	expedition.
	Any	court	in	which	a	proceeding	is	instituted
under	K.S.A.	44-1021	or	44-1022	shall	assign	the	case	for	hearing	at	the	earliest
practicable	date	and	cause	the	case	to	be	in	every	way	expedited.
History:
 L.	1970,	ch.	193,	§	9;	July	1.
