2025	Kansas	Statutes
44-1028.
 
Same;	severability.
	If	any	provision	of	this	act	or	the	application	thereof	to	any
person	or	circumstances	is	held	invalid,	the	remainder	of	the	act	and	the	application	of
the	provision	to	other	persons	not	similarly	situated	or	to	other	circumstances	shall	not
be	affected	thereby.
History:
 L.	1970,	ch.	193,	§	14;	July	1.
