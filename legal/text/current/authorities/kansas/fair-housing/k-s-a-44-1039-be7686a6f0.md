2025	Kansas	Statutes
44-1039.
 Unlawful	act;	perjury.
	Any	person	intentionally	and	falsely	swearing,	testifying,
affirming,	declaring	or	subscribing	to	any	material	fact	upon	any	oath	or	affirmation
required	by	the	Kansas	act	against	discrimination	shall	be	deemed	guilty	of	perjury	as
defined	in	K.S.A.	21-5903,	and	amendments	thereto.
History:
 L.	1975,	ch.	264,	§	5;	L.	2011,	ch.	30,	§	189;	July	1.
