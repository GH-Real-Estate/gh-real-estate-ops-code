2025	Kansas	Statutes
44-1033.
 
Initiation	and	processing	complaints;	use	of	information.
	If	the	compliance	review
conducted	by	the	commission	reveals	any	violation	of	the	Kansas	act	against
discrimination,	the	commission	may	initiate	a	complaint	and	process	such	complaint	in
the	manner	provided	for	processing	complaints	of	unlawful	employment	practices.	The
information	gathered	in	the	course	of	the	compliance	review	may	be	used	in	processing
the	complaint.
History:
 L.	1972,	ch.	194,	§	17;	July	1.
